Questions planners ask

How much does WeddingLedger cost for wedding planners?

The Planner plan is a one-time £40 payment that includes 3 client weddings with every Pro feature. Additional weddings are £12 each, one-time. There's no monthly subscription — you own it.

Can my couples manage their own guest list?

Yes. For each wedding you create a private link and auto-generated password for the couple. They add guests, set meal choices and track RSVPs themselves — no account needed — and everything they do appears in your dashboard instantly.

How do guests RSVP?

Every guest gets a personal invitation link. They open it, accept or decline, add dietary needs and their plus one — and the reply appears in real time for both you and the couple. You can even send links straight to WhatsApp.

Does each wedding keep its own budget and currency?

Yes. Each wedding has its own vendors, payments, remaining balances, due-date reminders and budget tracker, in that couple's own currency — GBP, USD, EUR, AUD or LKR.

Is my clients' data private?

Yes. Every wedding is isolated to your account with database-level security, and the couple's shared page is protected by its own password that you can regenerate or disable at any time.
